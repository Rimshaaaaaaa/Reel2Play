Privacy policy

Reel2Play. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Reel2Play turns a shop's product video into a short playable ad. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to have it deleted.

What shop owners give us

The video or photos you upload (only content you own and confirm), your shop name, offer, price, optional website link and your WhatsApp number. We use these only to make, test and publish your game, and to send you its link and results on WhatsApp. The original audio is muted, and faces of people who are not the product are blurred.

What players give us

Nothing personal. Games count anonymous events (started, finished, won, order tap) with no name, phone number, cookie ID or advertising ID. A share link carries only a score. A win code is random and not tied to the player.

WhatsApp messages

When you message our WhatsApp number we keep the chat needed to make your game. Players may join a shop's offers list only by sending JOIN themselves; sending STOP removes them at once. We never send unrequested messages.

Who processes the data

Hosting (Render), storage (Supabase), AI models run on Baseten and Modal, and WhatsApp messaging (Meta). They process data only to run Reel2Play. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Uploads and games are kept while your shop uses Reel2Play. Logs hold no personal data. Media links are signed and expire.

Deleting your data

Send STOP on WhatsApp to leave any offers list. To delete your shop, videos, games and chats, email rimsha.tariq1419@gmail.com with your shop name and WhatsApp number. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by reply.
